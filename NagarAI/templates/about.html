NagarAI

AI-assisted public infrastructure monitoring and governance decision support.

How the system works
  1. A citizen submits a civic complaint.
  2. The NLP model predicts a complaint category.
  3. A transparent scoring engine estimates priority using severity, public impact, and affected population.
  4. The complaint enters the dashboard for human review.
  5. An authorized official updates the case until it is resolved.
This prototype is designed for decision support. AI output should be reviewed by authorized officials and should not be the sole basis for high-impact public decisions.
